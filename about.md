# Bulk Chest Open

Tired of clicking through demon chests one by one? **Bulk Chest Open** lets you select and open multiple chests at once!

## How to Use

1. Hold **Shift** and click on chests to select them (they'll be highlighted in green with a checkmark)
2. Click the **"Open All"** button that appears, or click any selected chest
3. Watch a combined chest animation and see a detailed summary of all your rewards!

## Features

- **Multi-select chests** - Shift+Click to select as many chests as you want
- **Visual feedback** - Selected chests are highlighted with a green tint and checkmark
- **Combined rewards summary** - See all your orbs, diamonds, demon keys, shards, and unlocked items at once
- **Time saver** - No more clicking through dozens of chest animations individually

## Reward Summary

After bulk opening, you'll see a complete breakdown of:
- Total Orbs collected
- Total Diamonds earned
- Demon Keys obtained
- Shard totals by type
- Any special items unlocked

Perfect for players who have accumulated many demon keys and want to open all their chests quickly!
